# Can't help falling in love

## Paroles Anglaises

Wise men say  
Only fools rush in

Shall I stay?  
Would it be a sin

Like a river flows  
Surely to the sea  
Darling, so it goes  
Some things are meant to be  

Take my hand  
Take my whole life, too  
For I can't help  
falling in love with you
